Study method

Preparation guide

A practical sequence that works across vendors and subjects, from the first reading of the objectives through to the final week before your exam date.

Start with the published objectives

Download the official outline and turn every bullet into a checklist item. Rate your confidence per item and let the low scores decide where your study hours go.

Build a schedule you can keep

Short, frequent sessions beat long weekend marathons. Reserve named topics for each session, protect the slot in your calendar and keep the final week for revision.

Understand concepts before memorising

If you can explain a topic in plain language to someone else, you understand it. Memorised facts without context collapse as soon as a question is reworded.

Treat official documentation as the source of truth

Vendor documentation reflects current behaviour and the exact terminology used in questions. Verify anything you learned from a secondary source against it.

Practise hands on wherever possible

Labs, trial environments and sandboxes turn theory into memory. Configure, break and repair a feature and you will recognise it instantly under exam pressure.

Use practice questions responsibly

Questions are for diagnosis, not for shortcuts. Review the reasoning behind every answer, including the ones you got right, and never rely on memorised answer keys.

Work on weak areas deliberately

Keep a mistake log with the topic and the cause. Review it weekly, rebuild the topic from source material, then retest after a short gap to confirm the fix.

Run timed sessions

Full length timed practice builds pacing and stamina. Track how long each question type takes you and learn when to flag an item and move on.

Track progress honestly

Record scores, topics covered and confidence ratings. Visible progress keeps motivation steady and shows clearly when you are ready rather than merely hopeful.

Revisit hard topics on a cycle

Difficult subjects need several passes spaced days apart. Each return should be shorter than the last, ending with a quick summary written entirely from memory.

Plan the final preparation week

Consolidate notes, review the outline one last time, complete a timed session early in the week, confirm exam logistics and rest properly the night before.

Keep expectations realistic

No resource can promise a result. Steady preparation, genuine understanding and honest self assessment are what make exam day predictable and manageable.
